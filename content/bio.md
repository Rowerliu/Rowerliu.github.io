I am a Research Fellow in Biomedical Engineering at the National University of Singapore. I received my PhD in Biomedical Engineering from Beihang University in 2026. My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and biomedicine, with a particular focus on computational pathology. I develop generative and vision-language models for whole-slide image analysis, virtual staining, structured report generation, and pathologist-in-the-loop clinical AI systems. My broader goal is to translate advanced AI methods into reliable, interpretable, and clinically useful tools for digital medicine.
